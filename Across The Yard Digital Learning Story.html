Stop and Think 1 · Human Dignity

Who is Mia's neighbour? What did Jayden's words say about her worth?

Connect the scripture to the story in three steps. Open Mark 12:28-34 and Romans 14 if you want to check the passages.

Locked: Check your thinking (write an answer in all three boxes first)
  1. The scripture says: the second greatest commandment is to love our neighbour as ourselves, and Paul says we should welcome others and not look down on them.
  2. Human Dignity teaches: every person is made in God's image and has worth that cannot be taken away, so nothing anyone says can change it.
  3. In our story: everyone in the yard is Jayden's neighbour, including Mia. Mocking her drawing treated her as if she was worth less, but her worth hasn't changed. The whispering and the silence didn't love her as a neighbour either.

Stop and Think 2 · Human Dignity

Where does God see Mia? What does love look like?

Use the same three steps with the Beatitudes (Matthew 5:1-12) and Paul's words about love (1 Corinthians 13). This time, write the starters yourself.

Locked: Check your thinking (write an answer in all three boxes first)
  1. The scripture says: in the Beatitudes Jesus says God is close to people who are humble and hurting, such as those who mourn and the meek. Paul says love is patient and kind, not rude or boastful.
  2. Human Dignity teaches: every person has worth from God, and people who are hurting deserve extra care and respect.
  3. In our story: God sees Mia as blessed and valued even while she is being mocked. Jayden's actions were not patient, kind or respectful, so they didn't match the love Paul describes.

Test the story: does it protect or harm dignity?

Think about love being patient and kind, then choose one for each action from the story so far.

Stop and Think 3 · Guidance and Responsibility

Whose responsibility is Jayden's choice? What does good guidance teach us?

Paul says parents should bring children up in the “discipline and instruction of the Lord” (Ephesians 6:4). Think about the adults who guide young people, such as parents, teachers and coaches. Write about the story and the scripture, not your own family.

This time, name the Catholic Social Teaching yourself in step 2.

Locked: Check your thinking (write an answer in all three boxes first)
  1. The scripture says: parents should bring children up in the “discipline and instruction of the Lord,” which means guidance that teaches us how to live and how to treat other people.
  2. The Catholic Social Teaching is Human Dignity: every person is made in God's image, and good guidance helps us learn to treat everyone as having that worth.
  3. In our story: Jayden's mates pressured him, and Ms Rivera's teaching helped him see Mia's worth. But guidance can't make choices for us. Jayden was still responsible for what he did, and he chose to stop. Guidance, pressure and choice all matter, and the choice is still ours.

Frame 13 of 16
The Common Good A community works well when everyone has what they need to belong and flourish. When one person is hurt or left out, the whole community is affected, so we all share responsibility for each other.

Stop and Think 4 · The Common Good

How does a community look after everyone? What will you do?

Choose one scripture from the story: Luke 10:25-37, Hebrews 10:25, Acts 2:41-45 or Matthew 5:1-12. Explain how it connects to the Common Good in the story. This time there are no steps to help you, so plan your own answer: the scripture, the Catholic Social Teaching, and what happened in the story.

Locked: Check your thinking (write an answer in both boxes first)

Example answer: In Luke 10:25-37, a priest and a Levite cross the road, but the Samaritan stops and cares for the injured man. The Catholic Social Teaching of the Common Good says everyone in a community is responsible for each other's wellbeing, so that everyone can flourish. In our story, Sam first stays silent, like the people who crossed the road. Then he stops, sits with Mia and encourages her. Others follow, and the whole yard becomes a kinder community.

Example plan: Tomorrow I would notice anyone sitting alone and invite them to join us. I would speak up kindly if someone was being put down, and I would tell a teacher if it wasn't safe to do it myself.
